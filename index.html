Cleared to Land

What happens on the table happens in the sky.

Out now on Meta Quest Free in Early Access

You're the air traffic controller in a glass tower high above the airport. In front of you sits a living tabletop model of the airfield. Reach in, grab an arriving aircraft and draw its flight path through the air with your controller. Then look up: outside your windows, the full-size airliner banks along the exact line you drew, all the way down to the runway.

Get it right and they touch down safely. Get it wrong and two tiny planes collide on your table while a fireball lights up the sky outside.

Features

  • Draw in 3D. Paths have altitude: climb one jet over another, line a business jet up with its runway, drop a helicopter onto its pad.
  • Two worlds, one airport. Everything on the table happens at full scale outside your tower, at the same moment.
  • Six airfields, six times of day, from a morning countryside strip to a dusk metropolis with crossing runways.
  • Career and Survival modes, with global leaderboards for every airfield.
  • A hands-on tutorial that walks you through every move.
  • Comfortable to play seated or standing.

For Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S. Touch controllers required.
